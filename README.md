# Campus Rides

A ride-sharing board for college students who travel between campus, nearby cities, and home.

## Project Goal
College students who have to travel regionally, between cities, and home don't have many good options that balance cost, travel time, and schedules (I've taken 6 greyhounds so far this semester). Many students drive the same routes with empty seats in their car. There isn't a good way for drivers and riders to find each other, so the seats are unused. Campus rides lets students post and join rides in one place. 

## MVP Scope
**Included**
- Sign up and log in
- Drivers post trips with origin, destination, departure time, seats, and price
- Riders browse trips, filter by destination and date, and request a seat
- Drivers accept or decline requests, and seat counts update automatically
- Contact info is revealed only after a request is accepted
- A "My Trips" page for both riders and drivers

**Not included in the MVP**
- Payments
- In-app chat
- Maps and live tracking
